## Spec
`e2e/tests/narration-redo.spec.ts` is part of the project's Playwright suite (Chromium, runs in CI) and uses the stubbed backend from `playwright.config.ts`. The steps:
1. Run a session to its final video (Version 1).
2. Open scene 1's details, then "Redo scene 1 narration". The form shows the text, "83 characters" and the cost line. Confirm with "Read scene 1 again".
3. Wait for the "New narration" region. The preview `<audio>` has `preload="none"` and plays for real (`currentTime` passes 0.2 s).
4. "Keep new narration": the region closes, "Narration redone" shows, and the final video is rebuilt to Version 2.
5. A second take, then "Discard": the region closes, and the page still shows Version 2, the badge, and no rebuild.

The stub voice gives 1 s of audio, so a stubbed session has one scene. A range of several scenes is covered by the component tests (`frontend/test/narration-redo.test.tsx`).
